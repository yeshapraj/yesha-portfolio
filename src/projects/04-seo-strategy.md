At VML I supported an enterprise-level organic search strategy for **Ford.com**,
translating search data into recommendations that improved discoverability and
strengthened content performance across the customer journey.

The work blended technical and content-focused SEO: conducting keyword and
competitive research, identifying gaps and opportunities, and turning findings
into clear, actionable guidance for content, UX and cross-channel teams.

Because the client work is confidential, this portfolio focuses on the strategic
process, how I used search intent, performance insights and market analysis to
drive smarter content decisions.
