In 2025 I competed in the **Cannes Young Lions** competition, developing a fully
integrated campaign for WhyHunger in partnership with Whole Foods Market.

Our concept, **"Grow Your Garden"**, targeted Gen Z and Millennials by connecting
sustainable food education with measurable social impact.

Within 48 hours we built a comprehensive media plan, creative strategy and
participant journey rooted in sustainability, transparency and community
engagement.
