Fuelled the acceleration of UCB's **CIMZIA** product by conducting research,
analysing data, designing competitive insight reports, and working with the brand
team and agency partners to pressure-test and refine the 2022 CIMZIA Acceleration
plan.

Conducted SWOT analyses to understand the competitive market landscape.
Collaborated with the brand team and cross-functional partners to enhance
CIMZIA's value proposition, refine patient profiles, and strategise for the
Dermatology Portfolio's future development.

Assisted in Review Committee priorities and submissions to help prepare for a
pending brand launch, and supported the strategic planning and digital marketing
components for **Bimzelx** (bimekizumab).
