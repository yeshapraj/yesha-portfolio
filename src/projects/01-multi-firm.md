## The situation

Thirty-five accounting and tax firms, each with its own market, its own clients
and its own way of doing things — but all sharing one brand. Before this role
there was no single place the marketing came from, so every office was solving
the same problems separately.

## What I built

A set of templates and workflows that work the same way whoever is using them.
Campaign assets that drop into any firm's market without being rebuilt. A
publishing workflow in WordPress that cut the number of review rounds. Nurture
flows in HubSpot so a lead that comes in on a Friday is still being followed up
on the Tuesday.

**The test I use:** if it only works when I'm the one doing it, it isn't finished.

## Where it ended up

The firms publish faster and the messaging holds together across markets.
Leads stop falling through the gap between marketing and sales.
