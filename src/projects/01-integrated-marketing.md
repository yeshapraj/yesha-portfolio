## The situation

Thirty-five accounting and tax firms, each with its own market, its own clients
and its own way of working — but all sharing one brand. Every office was solving
the same problems separately.

## What I built

Templates and workflows that work the same way whoever is using them. Campaign
assets that drop into any firm's market without being rebuilt. A publishing
workflow in WordPress that cut the review rounds. Nurture flows in HubSpot so a
lead that arrives on a Friday is still being followed up on the Tuesday.

**The test I use:** if it only works when I'm the one doing it, it isn't finished.

## Where it ended up

Quarterly HubSpot newsletters, social campaigns running across the network, and
webpages and case studies that the firms can actually keep current.
