## Campaign and service collateral

Each service line needed its own material — tax credits, bookkeeping,
construction, advisory — written so a prospect outside the industry could follow
it. The 45L tax credit piece, the cost segregation explainer, the general
"providing solutions to help business grow" brochure.

## Affiliate firm brand alignment

Every firm in the network keeps its own name and local recognition while sitting
under the Archer Lewis brand. The rollout work was making both things true at
once — recognisable as part of the group, still recognisable in its own town.
