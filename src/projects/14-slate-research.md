Led a consumer research initiative evaluating purchase drivers and perception
gaps within the RTD beverage category, focusing on positioning opportunities for
Slate.

## Key insight

Consumers prioritised **taste over functional energy claims**, signalling a need
for messaging that emphasises flavour experience and packaging appeal rather than
performance positioning.

## Outcome

Presented data-backed recommendations for brand positioning, pricing and
competitive differentiation.

*Tools: Qualtrics, Excel.*
